Summary of open source etiquette
There are many reasons to contribute to an OSP
To improve your skills, to demonstrate and to help other people
We shall try  to help other contributors and congratulate them rather than judging them.We can find all the rules for an OSP
In the CODE_OF_CONDUCT.MD on github. Misbehaviors like hate speech, being rude, are followed by a warning and more serious ones likely result in a ban.Every fix is useful, no matter how small, and we won't turn any away. Nevertheless, try to make sure your fixes are productive.we shall read style guides before trying to change something.before asking questions,Try to find answers yourself as it may create unnecessary noise . To avoid noise 
Discuss one topic at a time,Fix one issue per PR,Read the manual first to try to answer the question yourself before asking it.
Dont cram multiple fixes into a single pull request.OSPs are quite democratic, decisions are voted on, and you are largely free to contribute how you want, as long as you don't impede anyone else from contributing.It is useful to get to know any project's moderators.we shall at least a day or two before taking follow ups as open source contributions are useably done by people in free time without any payment.

Additional points
Comment your code wherever necessary to explain it to the contributors 
Work on a separate branch instead of pushing directly to main.
Explain what your changes do in a good way.
Participate in issue discussions.
